# KILL-FARM-UPGRADE

An 2D pixel action incremental game made in Godot.

**How it works:** Kill enemies, earn coins & crystals, upgrade, repeat.

---

## Controls
- Move:  A / D <br>
- Jump:  W <br>
- Attack:  Left Click <br>
- Dash:  Shift <br>

---

## Features
- Enemy AI with chase, attack and stun
- Upgrade your stats
- Enemy Bosses with crazy attacks
- Upgrade your character
- Coins, crystals and kills tracked across runs
